# Northbridge Institute of Technology — Student Fact Sheet (2026–27)

Northbridge Institute of Technology (NIT-B) is a fictional private engineering college used for this evaluation.

## General

- Founded in 1998.
- Campus: 42 acres in Rivermoor, about 18 km from Rivermoor Central railway station.
- Approximately 4,200 students are enrolled.
- Director: Dr. Meera Kulkarni.
- Accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA) for all B.Tech programmes.
- Academic year starts on 4 August 2026.

## Departments and programmes

- The institute has five departments: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE), Mechanical Engineering (ME), Civil Engineering (CE), and Mathematics and Humanities (MH).
- B.Tech (4 years) is offered in CSE, ECE, ME and CE.
- M.Tech (2 years) is offered only in CSE and ECE.
- The Mathematics and Humanities department teaches courses to all programmes but does not offer its own degree.
- B.Tech intake per year: CSE 180 seats, ECE 120 seats, ME 60 seats, CE 60 seats.
- M.Tech intake per year: 30 seats each in CSE and ECE.

## Admissions

- B.Tech admission is through the Northbridge Entrance Test (NET), held once a year in May.
- The NET 2027 application window is 1 January to 15 March 2027.
- NET application fee: ₹1,500.
- Minimum eligibility for B.Tech: 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics in Class 12.
- M.Tech admission requires a valid GATE score; there is no separate institute test for M.Tech.
- Lateral entry into the second year of B.Tech is available for diploma holders, with 10% of seats in each B.Tech programme reserved for it.

## Fees

- B.Tech tuition fee: ₹1,20,000 per semester.
- M.Tech tuition fee: ₹80,000 per semester.
- One-time admission fee (all programmes): ₹25,000.
- Refundable caution deposit: ₹10,000, returned after graduation.
- Fees can be paid in two instalments per semester; the second instalment carries a late charge of ₹2,000 if paid after the semester's mid-term exams.
- Fees are paid only through the student portal; cash is not accepted.

## Scholarships

- Merit scholarship: 50% tuition waiver for students ranked in the top 100 of NET.
- Need-based scholarship: 25% tuition waiver for students whose family income is below ₹6,00,000 per year.
- A student can receive only one institute scholarship at a time.
- To keep a scholarship, a student must maintain a CGPA of at least 7.5.

## Hostel and mess

- There are three hostels: Aravali (boys, 600 beds), Nilgiri (boys, 500 beds) and Kaveri (girls, 450 beds).
- All hostel rooms are non-AC and shared by two students.
- Hostel fee (non-AC, twin sharing): ₹45,000 per semester.
- Mess fee: ₹22,000 per semester, compulsory for all hostel residents.
- Hostel curfew: 10:30 PM for all hostels.
- First-year students are guaranteed a hostel room; seniors are allotted rooms by lottery.
- Wi-Fi is available in all hostel rooms.

## Library

- The Central Library has about 85,000 books and subscribes to IEEE Xplore and ScienceDirect.
- Library hours: 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Monday to Friday; 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on Saturday.
- The library is closed on Sundays.
- Undergraduate students can borrow up to 4 books at a time for 14 days.
- Postgraduate students can borrow up to 6 books at a time for 30 days.
- Late return fine: ₹5 per book per day.

## Academics and exams

- Each academic year has two semesters: Odd (August–December) and Even (January–May).
- Minimum attendance required to sit end-semester exams: 75%.
- Grading is on a 10-point CGPA scale.
- Minimum CGPA to graduate: 5.0.
- A student who fails a course can take a supplementary exam, held in June; the supplementary exam fee is ₹1,000 per course.
- End-semester exam results are published on the student portal within 30 days of the last exam.

## Placements

- The Training and Placement Cell is headed by Mr. Arjun Rao.
- Placement season for final-year students starts in August.
- 2025–26 placement statistics: 86% of eligible B.Tech students were placed; the median package was ₹7.2 lakh per year; the highest package was ₹42 lakh per year.
- Students need a CGPA of at least 6.0 to register with the Placement Cell.
- A student who accepts one job offer cannot sit for further placement drives.

## Campus facilities

- Sports: a football ground, two basketball courts, an indoor badminton hall and a 25-metre swimming pool.
- The swimming pool is open from 6:00 AM to 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM.
- Medical centre: open 24 hours, with a doctor on duty from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
- College buses run from Rivermoor Central railway station; the bus pass costs ₹9,000 per semester.
- The campus has an ATM of State Commerce Bank near the main gate.

## Student life

- The annual technical festival, TechNova, is held in February.
- The annual cultural festival, Utsav, is held in October.
- There are 24 registered student clubs, including a robotics club, a coding club and a music club.
- Ragging is strictly prohibited; complaints go to the Anti-Ragging Committee helpline 1800-200-4455.
